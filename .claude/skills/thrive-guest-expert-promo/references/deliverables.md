# Deliverable specs

Jude's original directions are quoted in *italics*. The notes beneath each one are how to execute it well.

---

## 1. Talk title
*Create a talk title under 30 characters for this session.*
- Count every character, including spaces. Show the count, e.g. `Calm Your Energy, Come Home (27)`.
- Give one recommended title + 2 alternatives.
- Make it benefit-led and warm, not jargon-heavy. The guest's long original title can live on in the Eventbrite title/description.

## 2. Summary
*Create a summary under 300 characters including the main benefits participants will gain from the session.*
- One short paragraph and show the count. Name the guest, what happens, and 2–3 key benefits.

## 3. Promo key points
*Can you pull out additional info from this for creating promotional posts for this session?*

Give a bulleted bank under these headings:
- **Hooks / opening lines** (5–6)
- **Pain points it speaks to**: how the reader feels before
- **Benefits / how you may feel after**
- **Who it's for**
- **Reassurance lines**, e.g. "no experience needed", "nothing to do or achieve", cameras optional (only if true)
- **What happens in the session**: a simple step-by-step
- **Guest credibility points**: only from their bio
- **Quotable lines**: the guest's own words worth quoting
- **Key terms explained simply**: any jargon (e.g. "Akashic Records") in one plain sentence

## 4. Eventbrite
*Please create an SEO-optimised Eventbrite title, 1. a 150-character meta description, 2. a full event description emphasising what the participants will get out of the session, 3. Eventbrite tags/keywords. What FAQ could I add to Eventbrite?*

- **SEO title** (aim for under ~75 characters; put the main search phrase first). Pattern: `[Searchable topic]: [Benefit] | [Format] with [Guest]`, e.g. `Akashic Records Healing: Calm Your Nervous System | Online Session`.
- **Meta description**: ≤150 characters, with the count shown. Include the main keyword plus a benefit plus "online"/"live".
- **Full description** structure:
  1. An opening hook (2 sentences, speaking to how they feel now)
  2. About the session
  3. **What you'll receive / how you may feel**, in ✦ bullets
  4. What happens in the session (a simple flow)
  5. Who it's for, and "no experience needed" if true
  6. About [Guest] (rewritten bio, 80–120 words)
  7. About Thrive Together Circle (2–3 sentences: a women's empowerment community, growing through life's transitions, seen, heard and supported)
  8. Practical details: date, time + time zone, length, platform (Skool), what to bring (quiet space, headphones, water, journal)
  9. A warm closing invitation
- **Tags**: Eventbrite allows up to 10 tags. Give 10, mixing topic, benefit and audience (e.g. `women's wellbeing`, `online healing`). Then give 10–15 extra SEO keywords to weave into the text.
- **FAQs**: give 6–10 Q&As. Always cover: Do I need experience? Where is it held / how do I join? Is it recorded / is there a replay? (`[CONFIRM]` unless known). What do I need? Do I need my camera on? Who is it for? Is it suitable if I'm [emotional state]? Plus topic-specific ones. For any healing/energy session, add one gentle line saying it complements, and doesn't replace, medical or psychological care.

## 5. Social posts — Skool, Facebook, LinkedIn
*You are a social media expert and strategist. Create social media posts to promote within my Skool group (where the session will take place) and on Facebook and LinkedIn, promoting this as another amazing session within the Thrive Together Circle, a women's empowerment community, supporting you in your life and business. A community created for women who want to grow through life's transitions, not just survive them. A space to be seen, heard and supported. Ask in the post in different ways to join Thrive Together Circle, or use "link in comments" if better.*

**Skool (2–3 posts)**: these are for existing members, inside the community where the session happens.
- A post title (Skool shows titles), e.g. "✨ Next guest session: ..."
- Content: an announcement, plus what to expect, plus the date/time and "add it to your calendar" (Skool events are on the Calendar tab), plus an engagement question ("What would you love to ask Sandra?" / "Drop a 💛 if you're joining").
- Variations: announcement, 1-day reminder, and "we're live in 1 hour / starting now".
- Encourage members to invite a friend to the Circle.

**Facebook (3 posts)**: for a personal profile / page and groups.
- Variations: a story-led post (Jude's voice, relatable moment), a benefit-led post (✦ bullets) and a short punchy post.
- The first 1–2 lines must hook before the "See more" cut.
- CTA: "link in comments", and supply the **first comment** text. Each post uses a different invitation to join.
- 3–5 hashtags at most.

**LinkedIn (2 posts)**: professional but still warm.
- Angle: women in business, burnout, leadership through transitions, and a community of support for your life *and* business.
- A short hook line, white space, ✦ or → bullets, and 3–5 hashtags.
- Suggest tagging the guest. Put the link in the first comment.

Label each post clearly (e.g. **Facebook post 2 — benefit-led**) and add a suggested posting time relative to the event.

## 6. Reels — Instagram & TikTok
*You are a social media expert and strategist for short-form content... The highest-performing Reels start with a strong hook in the first 3 seconds, create an emotional connection, and finish with a clear CTA. 30–45 second talking-head videos with captions and B-roll. Position Thrive Together Circle as the hero rather than the event itself. Each reel highlights the community's mission (helping women grow through life's transitions with expert guidance, genuine connection and ongoing support) while showcasing the session as one of the many reasons to become part of the community.*

Write **3 reel scripts**, each with a different angle, e.g.:
1. "You don't have to do this alone": the community is the hero
2. Pain point → relief: the session is the example
3. "What happens inside Thrive Together Circle": the session is one of the many things that happen there

Format each one as follows:
- **Hook (0–3s)**: the spoken line, plus the on-screen text
- A **script table** with columns `Time | Jude says (to camera) | On-screen caption | B-roll idea`
- **CTA** (last 5s): the spoken line, plus the on-screen text
- Estimated length (count about 2.5 spoken words per second; keep it to 75–110 words spoken)
- **Instagram caption** (with "comment CIRCLE for the link" or "link in bio") + 5–8 hashtags
- **TikTok caption** (shorter) + 3–5 hashtags
- A suggested trending-style audio *mood* (don't name specific copyrighted tracks)
