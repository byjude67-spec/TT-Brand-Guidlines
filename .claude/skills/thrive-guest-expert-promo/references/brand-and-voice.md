# Thrive Together Circle — Brand & Voice

Source: *Thrive Together Circle Brand Guidelines* by Jude Hill (this repo's `index.html`).

## The community (use this positioning in every piece)
- **Thrive Together Circle** is a women's empowerment community, founded by Jude Hill, that supports you in your life and business.
- It's *a community created for women who want to grow through life's transitions, not just survive them.*
- It's *a space to be seen, heard and supported.*
- It's hosted on **Skool**, where the guest sessions take place.
- Pillars: **Clarity, Connection and Support.**
- Jude leads from lived experience as a mum of 4, grief coach and energy healer.
- Membership (mention only if relevant): 🌱 Founding Member (Free) · 🌸 Circle Member ($97/mo) · ✨ 1:1 with Jude (Discovery Call). Don't quote prices unless Jude confirms they're current.

Guest sessions are framed as **"another amazing session inside Thrive Together Circle"**. Each one is a reason to join, and evidence that the community brings real expert guidance, genuine connection and ongoing support.

## Voice principles
1. **Warm, not saccharine.** Genuine care without hollow positivity.
2. **Encouraging, not prescriptive.** "You might try..." not "You must...". Give permission, not orders.
3. **Honest about the hard stuff.** Growth isn't linear. Name it and hold space for it.
4. **No perfection required.** Progress over perfect. This is the heartbeat of the brand.
5. **Grounded in lived experience.** Real words for real women navigating real life.

Jude speaks like a trusted friend who genuinely wants the best for you. She's never pushy and never performative. Use UK spelling (centred, colour, recognise, mum). Use emojis sparingly; 🌸 💛 ✨ 🌱 ☀️ fit the brand.

### Write this ✓
- "How are you really doing this week? Not the 'I'm fine' version — the real one."
- "No win is too tiny. Getting out of bed counts. Choosing yourself counts."
- "I've got you all. I'll be back Friday to hear how you got on."

### Avoid this ✗
- "Unlock your full potential and step into your best self today!"
- "You NEED to do this. Don't miss out — limited spots available!"
- "Follow these 5 proven steps to transform your life in 30 days."
- Also avoid: fake scarcity/countdown pressure, "game-changer", "level up", "hustle", guaranteed results, and medical claims.

### Ways to invite people to join (rotate them, never repeat across posts)
- "Come and join us inside Thrive Together Circle 🌸 [link]"
- "The link to join the Circle is in the comments 💛"
- "Comment CIRCLE and I'll send you the link."
- "If this speaks to you, there's a seat saved for you in the Circle."
- "Not in the Circle yet? This is your sign. Link below."
- "Already a member? Pop a 💛 below so I know you're coming."
- "Tag a friend who needs this kind of support right now."

On Facebook and LinkedIn, links in the post body reduce reach. Prefer "link in the comments" and give the first comment text too.

## Visual identity
**Fonts (only these two):** DM Serif Display for headlines and DM Sans for everything else.
**Headline signature:** exactly ONE italic word per headline, in gold `#E8C060` on gradients or magenta `#E8196A` on light backgrounds.

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Vivid magenta (primary CTA) | `#E8196A` |
| Pink | `#F0508A` |
| Orange (secondary CTA) | `#F07820` |
| Golden yellow (sun rays) | `#F2B830` |
| Logo orange | `#E8902A` |
| Gold (metallic thread) | `#C9952A` |
| Gold light (italic word on gradient) | `#E8C060` |
| Blush background | `#FFF5F8` |
| Headlines on light | `#8B1A4A` |
| Body on light | `#6B2A3A` |
| Subtitles | `#A0506A` |
| Text on vivid backgrounds | `#FFE8F0` |

- **Primary gradient:** magenta → pink → orange (`#E8196A → #F0508A → #F07820`), diagonal.
- **Alt gradient:** orange → golden → magenta.
- **Gold thread overlay** on gradients only: `repeating-linear-gradient(45deg, rgba(201,149,42,0.20) ...)`. Never on light backgrounds.
- **On gradients:** white headlines, body text at `rgba(255,255,255,0.78)`, labels at `rgba(255,255,255,0.72)`.
- **Buttons:** pill shape (50px radius), DM Sans 700.
- **Logo:** only on blush, white or brand gradients. On gradients, put it in a frosted ring (`rgba(255,255,255,0.18)`). Never stretch, recolour or crowd it.
