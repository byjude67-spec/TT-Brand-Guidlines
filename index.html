06 — Communication

Voice & tone

Nurturing, honest, grounded. Jude speaks like a trusted friend who genuinely wants the best for you — never pushy, never performative.

Voice principles
01
Warm, not saccharine
Genuine care without hollow positivity. Real words for real women who are navigating real life.
02
Encouraging, not prescriptive
Invite rather than instruct. "You might try..." not "You must..." Give permission, not orders.
03
Honest about the hard stuff
Growth isn't always linear. Name that, hold space for it, and never pretend otherwise.
04
No perfection required
Progress over perfect, always. Say it often. Model it yourself. It's the heartbeat of the brand.
05
Grounded in lived experience
Jude leads from what she's actually lived — as a mum of 4, grief coach and energy healer — not from theory.
Do and don't
Write this
"How are you really doing this week? Not the 'I'm fine' version — the real one."
Write this
"No win is too tiny. Getting out of bed counts. Choosing yourself counts."
Write this
"I've got you all. I'll be back Friday to hear how you got on."
Avoid this
"Unlock your full potential and step into your best self today!"
Avoid this
"You NEED to do this. Don't miss out — limited spots available!"
Avoid this
"Follow these 5 proven steps to transform your life in 30 days."
Key taglines
Clarity, Connection and Support.
"When life changes, you don't have to figure it out alone."
"Real growth. No perfection."
"A space for real growth, real connection, real support."
Use the primary tagline consistently across all touchpoints. Secondary phrases can rotate in content but must feel like natural extensions of the same warm, grounded voice.
